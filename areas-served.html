Communities We Serve

Every community in Summit County has its own character. Here's what we know about the homeowners, and homes, in each area we serve.

Hudson

Summit County's premier community, historic architecture, top-rated schools, and some of the most thoughtfully maintained homes in Northeast Ohio. Hudson homeowners are typically strategic: they've been in their homes for years, they know they want to invest in the property, and they want a plan that's worthy of the home.

We've assessed dozens of Hudson homes across a wide range of styles. Victorian and Greek Revival historics on East Main Street, ranches and colonials in established subdivisions, and newer construction on the perimeter. The Home Clarity Report is particularly valuable in Hudson because the density of home styles means project pricing varies significantly. A kitchen in a 1920 farmhouse and a kitchen in a 2005 colonial are fundamentally different scopes. Knowing the difference before you hire anyone is worth a great deal.

Median home value: approximately $508,000

Read the Hudson neighborhood guide →

Bath Township

Sprawling properties and premium construction. Bath Township is one of Summit County's most prestigious areas, large lots, strong resale values, and homeowners who expect the highest quality in everything they do with their property.

Bath Township projects often involve significant scope: full first-floor transformations, master suite additions, substantial exterior work on large homes. The scale of potential investment in Bath makes the Home Clarity Report particularly valuable, getting the sequence and pricing right on a $200,000 renovation plan is worth far more than $4,500 to get wrong.

Median home value: approximately $518,000

Read the Bath Township neighborhood guide →

Fairlawn

One of Summit County's highest-activity renovation markets. Fairlawn homeowners tend to be active planners, they have clear project ideas, they're ready to spend money, and they want to make sure every dollar is placed correctly. Kitchen and bathroom work dominates here, along with first-floor opens and finishing projects that update 1980s and 1990s interiors.

Fairlawn is also close enough to Montrose and Summit Mall that homeowners have strong exposure to what premium finished products look like, which raises their expectations for their own renovations. The Home Clarity Report helps Fairlawn homeowners bridge the gap between what they want and what's realistic at their budget before any contractor conversation begins.

Read the Fairlawn neighborhood guide →

Richfield

Quiet, premium, and positioned between Cleveland and Akron with strong access to both. Richfield homeowners tend to be the strategic type, they think long-term about their property, they're not in a rush, and they want to invest in the right projects in the right order.

The 10-year plan approach resonates strongly in Richfield. Clients here often use the Home Clarity Report to map out a decade of improvements rather than react to immediate needs. The Report's sequencing guidance is particularly valuable for homeowners who want to build strategically rather than renovate reactively.

Book a discovery call for your Richfield home →

Montrose-Ghent (44333)

One of Summit County's highest-income zip codes, and home to some of our most detail-oriented clients. Montrose-Ghent homeowners want specifics: exact pricing, clear scopes, and a process they can trust. They've often had experiences with contractors who overpromised and underdelivered, and they're specifically looking for someone with a different approach.

The Home Clarity Report's transparent pricing and written project scopes resonate strongly here because Montrose-Ghent clients are exactly the audience that responds to specific numbers and documented commitments over vague estimates.

Read the Montrose-Ghent neighborhood guide →

Silver Lake

Waterfront living and tight-knit community. Silver Lake homeowners care deeply about their properties and their neighborhood, word travels fast here, and quality work is noticed and discussed. We've served Silver Lake clients across a range of project types, and the referral rate from this community is among the highest in our service area.

Book a discovery call for your Silver Lake home →

Stow

From established neighborhoods to newer builds, Stow has a wide variety of home types and a strong community of homeowners who are actively investing in their properties. Stow clients often come to us mid-project: they've started planning, gotten a few estimates, and realized they need a neutral expert to help them make sense of the numbers.

Read the Stow neighborhood guide →

Cuyahoga Falls

Our home base. We've been serving Cuyahoga Falls homeowners since 1999, these are our neighbors. The mix of home styles here is wide: Merriman Valley properties along the river, established ranches in the interior neighborhoods, and older homes throughout the historic downtown corridor. We know this community at a level that comes from 27 years of working here.

Read the Cuyahoga Falls neighborhood guide →

Copley

Established neighborhoods and well-built homes. Copley homeowners appreciate quality craftsmanship and expect contractors to show up prepared. We serve Copley regularly and understand the range of home types, from the larger homes along Smith Road to the more modest neighborhoods throughout the township.

Book a discovery call for your Copley home →

Peninsula

Cuyahoga Valley National Park corridor, scenic, historic, and unlike anywhere else in Summit County. Peninsula homes have character and age, and they require contractors who understand historic construction, restoration techniques, and the specific challenges of older structures. Adam's experience with historic properties (including his own 1834 farmhouse restoration) makes the Home Clarity Report particularly well-suited for Peninsula homeowners.

Read the Peninsula neighborhood guide →

Tallmadge

A stable, well-kept Summit County community with a strong mix of post-war ranches and colonials, newer construction, and mid-century homes being actively updated. Tallmadge homeowners are practical, value-conscious investors in their properties, they want to make sure every dollar goes in the right direction before anyone picks up a tool.

HBC serves Tallmadge homeowners who are ready to invest in their home but aren't sure where to start. The Home Clarity Report gives them a documented foundation, prioritized sequence, realistic cost ranges, and written scopes, that makes every contractor conversation productive instead of confusing.
