Advisory Categories

What We Advise On

The Home Clarity Report covers every room and system in your home. These are the six advisory categories our clients most commonly engage with — each covered in detail, with current Northeast Ohio cost ranges, in every report we deliver.

Kitchen Renovation

Kitchen Assessment & Scope

We document your kitchen's current condition, assess the plumbing, electrical, and ventilation, and define a written scope of work matched to your goals. Trade partners receive this documentation before the first conversation — arriving with an accurate picture of the project, not a blank-slate estimate.

Typical range: $12,000 – $120,000+
Bathroom Renovation

Bathroom Assessment & Scope

Bathroom renovations have the widest gap between homeowner expectations and hidden realities — waterproofing issues, galvanized supply pipes, inadequate ventilation. We document what's behind the walls before a trade partner sees the project. That information transforms the contractor conversation and eliminates surprise discoveries during demo.

Typical range: $6,000 – $95,000+
HVAC & Mechanical Systems

Systems Assessment & Timeline

We assess system age, condition, last service documentation, and current efficiency. We document projected replacement timelines and current Summit County cost ranges for furnace, AC, and heat pump systems. HVAC trade partners receive this assessment before the site visit — enabling accurate quotes on properly scoped replacements.

Typical range: $4,500 – $22,000
Roofing

Roof Condition & Replacement Scope

We document roofing material, age, visible condition, attic ventilation, and gutter systems. We identify active moisture intrusion and assess whether repair or replacement is appropriate. Trade partners receive this documentation before their site estimate — which produces more accurate quotes and eliminates the "we found more damage than expected" conversation after work begins.

Typical range: $8,000 – $20,000
Basement Finishing

Basement Readiness Assessment

We assess the basement before any finishing work is specified — moisture penetration, drainage, sump system condition, structural concerns, and egress requirements. Finishing over an unaddressed moisture issue is the most expensive mistake in residential renovation. We document what needs to be resolved first, so trade partners aren't bidding on a project that will be undone in three years.

Typical range: $20,000 – $55,000
Full-Home Renovation Planning

Multi-Phase Renovation Roadmap

Homeowners with multiple simultaneous projects need a sequencing plan before any trade partner starts work. We create documented, phase-appropriate renovation plans with project sequencing, cost ranges by phase, and priority tiers. Trade partners benefit because the homeowner arrives pre-decided on project order — meaning your work fits into a plan rather than being replanned around you.

Scope varies by project count and complexity
