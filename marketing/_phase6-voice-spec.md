## Voice rules (CRITICAL)
- Sound like a human wrote it. Mix short and long sentences. Never start two sentences in a row the same way.
- **Never use:** em dashes (`—`, `–`, `&mdash;`, `&#8212;`, `&#x2014;`). Use commas or periods.
- **En-dash exception:** numeric ranges in pre-existing pricing are OK in the live site only, but new files MUST avoid all dashes other than hyphens.
- **Banned words:** luxury, high-end, delve, leverage, robust, seamlessly, moreover, furthermore, navigate
- **Do not use** "AI" anywhere except where Adam approves. Phase 6 default: write around it. Use phrases like "knows your house", "trained on your home and only your home", "Hub", "portal", "operating system for your house"
- Do not mention AK Renovations revenue
- Use "we", "our team" more than "Adam" alone — HBC is a team, Adam is the founder

## The Realtor Pitch (use these exact beats)
1. **The HBC stamp travels with the house.** Two stamps: Hometown Builders Club + Home Clarity Report. They show up in listings, lift home value, give buyers a documented reason to pick that house.
2. **HBC makes realtors the hero to past clients.** When a former client calls them with a roof leak, an HVAC issue, anything related to the house, the realtor has one number to give them: ours. Even if the homeowner never buys a Report, we still help. The realtor wins the relationship.
3. **Either door, both win.** If a client comes to the realtor first, they refer in for a Report. If a homeowner finds HBC first without a realtor, we send them to a partner from our trusted realtor list. We will never recommend another realtor to a homeowner who already has one.

## The Trade Pitch (use these exact 7 beats)

### What 15% buys you (one flat fee, billed on close, the entire fee structure)

1. **Sold jobs, not leads.** The homeowner has already decided to do the project. Scope written. Budget approved. They're ready to sign.
2. **A complete digital briefing before you arrive.** Floor plans accurate to 1/8 inch. 360° photos. Full system documentation. Written project scope from a 27-year remodeling expert.
3. **No competition.** You're the one trade we recommend for that project.
4. **The pricing is already set. You honor it.** When we hand you a sold job, the homeowner has already seen and accepted a budget, and that budget came from your own pricing data. You're not bidding the job. You're delivering it at the number we already quoted. No re-negotiation. No surprises for the homeowner. No awkward conversations on day one.
5. **A permanent record of your work.** Every project you complete for an HBC homeowner gets documented in their Home Clarity Hub. Photos, scope, materials, warranty terms, your name. When they sell the house in 12 years, your craftsmanship is part of what makes it more valuable.
6. **Pay only when the job closes.** No upfront fees. No pay-to-play. No monthly subscriptions. 15% of the contract value, billed when the homeowner signs. If a project doesn't close, you owe nothing.
7. **A vetted network. Not a free-for-all.** We screen, interview, and reference-check every trade in the network. The homeowner knows that. Your reputation gets a halo from being one of ours.
