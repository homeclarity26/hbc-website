## Application Section 4: Description of Services

### Brief Description of Services (Short, for directory listing)

Hometown Builders Club is Summit County, Ohio's home advisory and vetted trade partner network. We document how your home is built, what every project will cost, and who to call. Founded by Adam Kilgore, a licensed general contractor with 27 years and over 400 residential projects in Summit County.

### Full Description of Services

Hometown Builders Club (HBC) is a home advisory firm and vetted trade partner network based in Cuyahoga Falls, Ohio, serving Summit County homeowners since 1999. Our founder, Adam Kilgore, is a licensed Ohio General Contractor and EPA Lead Safe Certified Renovator who has personally worked in, remodeled, or built over 400 homes in Summit County over 27 years.

HBC's primary service is the Home Clarity Report: a comprehensive, on-site home assessment delivered by Adam personally within five business days. The Report includes interior floor plans accurate to 1/8 inch, a full 3D exterior model, 360-degree photography of every room, written project scopes with honest recommendations, realistic budget ranges drawn from 27 years of Summit County pricing data, condition ratings for every major mechanical system, and lifetime access to a private Home Clarity Hub portal. The portal serves as a permanent operating system for the home. It holds paint colors, system ages, maintenance records, warranty documents, and prior project scopes. The homeowner and their family can access it from any device, at any time, for as long as they own the home.

Beyond the Report, HBC maintains a vetted network of screened trade partners: plumbers, electricians, HVAC technicians, finish carpenters, tile setters, and general contractors. When a homeowner is ready to move forward on a project, HBC makes the introduction. The trade partner arrives with the homeowner's complete home profile already in hand. That informational advantage means faster conversations, more accurate bids, fewer change orders, and no surprises for the homeowner.

HBC also maintains relationships with Summit County realtors. We partner with agents to help buyers and sellers understand the true condition and potential of a home before they finalize any transaction.

Our service area is Summit County, Ohio, with priority neighborhoods including Hudson, Bath Township, Fairlawn, Richfield, Montrose-Ghent, Silver Lake, Merriman Valley, Stow, Copley, Cuyahoga Falls, and Peninsula.

## Application Section 8: Customer Service Philosophy

### Describe Your Customer Service Philosophy

We built Hometown Builders Club on one idea: homeowners making six-figure decisions deserve someone in their corner who doesn't benefit from what they decide. That means we never bid on work we assess. Our only incentive is being right.

Before a homeowner hires anyone, we want them to find us first. That is the core of how we operate. We give homeowners a complete, documented picture of their home before any contractor conversation begins. That removes the guesswork and the pressure. It also removes the conflict of interest that runs through the rest of the industry.

Once a homeowner is an HBC client, the relationship does not end. We are a lifetime advisor. New project, unexpected repair, contractor bid that needs a second look, we are one call away, forever, at no additional charge for advisory consultations. That is not a sales line. It is how we have operated for 27 years in Summit County, and it is why most of our new clients come from past clients.

We treat every homeowner's house the way we would treat a neighbor's house. Because in Summit County, it usually is.

## Application Section 9: Dispute Resolution Policy

### How Does Your Business Handle Customer Complaints and Disputes?

We resolve every issue directly with the homeowner. When a concern is raised, we acknowledge it in writing within one business day and work toward a resolution within five business days of the issue being raised. We do not pass complaints to a general inbox or a voicemail. Adam handles them personally.

If the concern involves a third-party trade partner we introduced to the homeowner, we mediate directly between the homeowner and the trade partner. We stand behind every introduction we make. If a trade partner we referred caused a problem, we are involved in the resolution. We make it right.

No concern ever requires a homeowner to submit a formal complaint to a third party before we have had the opportunity to address it directly.
