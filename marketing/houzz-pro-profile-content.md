### Long Bio (1,500 characters max)

*Character count: approx. 1,480. Paste as-is or trim from the bottom if Houzz rejects.*

Hometown Builders Club is Summit County, Ohio's home advisory and vetted trade network. We help homeowners understand how their home is built, what every project will actually cost, and who to call when the time comes. We have been doing this work in Summit County since 1999.

Our flagship service is the Home Clarity Report: a full on-site assessment delivered by our founder, Adam Kilgore, within five business days. The Report includes interior floor plans accurate to 1/8 inch, a 3D exterior model, 360-degree photography, written project scopes, honest budget ranges based on real Summit County pricing, and condition ratings for every major mechanical system. Everything gets stored in a private Home Clarity Hub portal that the homeowner and their family can access from any device, anytime.

Once the Report is delivered, we connect homeowners to our vetted trade network. Plumbers, electricians, HVAC technicians, finish carpenters, and general contractors arrive with the homeowner's complete home profile already in hand. No starting from scratch. No competing bids built on different guesses about the same scope.

We are a lifetime advisor. One call, forever, for every question that comes up after the Report is delivered.
