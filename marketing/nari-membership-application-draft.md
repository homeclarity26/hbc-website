## Section 12: Why You Want to Join NARI

*NARI asks applicants to explain their reason for seeking membership. Write this in your own words, or copy-paste the draft below and adjust if needed.*

I have spent 27 years working in Summit County homes, and one thing has stayed consistent: the standard of care that homeowners deserve is not always the standard they receive. NARI membership matters to me because it signals to homeowners, trade partners, and the broader community that we hold ourselves to a higher bar. Not just in craftsmanship, but in ethics, transparency, and how we treat the people who trust us with their biggest asset.

The partnership opportunities within NARI are a direct fit for what we are building at HBC. We work with a vetted network of trade contractors across Northeast Ohio, and connecting with other professionals who take their membership in NARI seriously means raising the quality of every referral we make and every introduction we facilitate.

Continued education is something our team takes seriously, too. The remodeling industry changes, materials change, codes change, and the best way to give homeowners accurate guidance is to stay current. NARI's educational programs give us a structured way to do that.

We are not joining NARI for a logo to put on a website. We are joining because it reflects the kind of company we are committed to being in this community.
