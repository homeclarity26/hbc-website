### Post 6 -- Friday, May 30, 2026
**Type:** FAQ Post
**Title:** "How Long Will My Remodel Take?" Here Is an Honest Answer

**Body:**
This is the question every homeowner asks and a lot of contractors fudge.

I am not going to tell you eight weeks when I mean sixteen. Here is how I actually think about timelines.

Bathrooms: A straightforward bath remodel runs 4 to 8 weeks in production. Custom tile work, relocated plumbing, or a larger footprint pushes it toward the higher end. Factor in 2 to 4 weeks for selection and ordering before we even start demo.

Kitchens: Plan for 8 to 14 weeks in production once the project starts. Semi-custom cabinets usually have a 6-8 week lead time. Full custom can be 10-12. That production phase does not begin until cabinets are ordered and materials are confirmed. Add selection time on top.

First floor projects: These vary the most. A layout change with flooring, trim, and lighting might run 10 to 16 weeks. Add a kitchen component and you are at 14 to 20.

What slows projects down? Hidden conditions behind walls, late material selections, changes to scope mid-project, and permit timing. I try to surface hidden conditions before we start by doing a Home Clarity Report first. It does not guarantee zero surprises, but it cuts them down significantly.

I give realistic schedules. Not the schedule I think you want to hear.
