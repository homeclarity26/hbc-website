One flat fee. Seven things you stop doing the day you join.

When a project closes, you pay HBC 15% of the contract value. That's the entire fee structure. No subscriptions, no upfront cost, no pay-to-play. Here's exactly what that 15% replaces.

01

Sold jobs, not leads.

The homeowner has already decided to do the project. The scope is written. The budget is approved. They're ready to sign. You stop chasing leads that were never going to convert.

02

A complete digital briefing before you arrive.

Floor plans accurate to 1/8 inch. 360° photos of every relevant space. Full system documentation. Written project scope from a 27-year remodeling expert. You walk in knowing the home better than most contractors do after three site visits.

03

No competition.

You're the one trade we recommend for that project. The homeowner isn't gathering five quotes. They're meeting their contractor.

04

The pricing is already set. You honor it.

When we hand you a sold job, the homeowner has already seen and accepted a budget, and that budget came from your own pricing data. You're not bidding the job. You're delivering it at the number we already quoted. No re-negotiation. No surprises for the homeowner. No awkward conversations on day one.

05

A permanent record of your work.

Every project you complete for an HBC homeowner gets documented in their Home Clarity Hub. Photos, scope, materials, warranty terms, your name. When they sell the house in 12 years, your craftsmanship is part of what makes it more valuable.

06

Pay only when the job closes.

No upfront fees. No pay-to-play. No monthly subscriptions. 15% of the contract value, billed when the homeowner signs. If a project doesn't close, you owe nothing.

07

A vetted network. Not a free-for-all.

We screen, interview, and reference-check every trade in the network. The homeowner knows that. Your reputation gets a halo from being one of ours, and the network gets stronger every time another good trade joins.
