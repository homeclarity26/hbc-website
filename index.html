QUICK ANSWERS

Plain answers to the questions homeowners ask us most.

What is Hometown Builders Club?

Hometown Builders Club is Summit County, Ohio's home advisory and vetted trade partner network. It connects homeowners with the Home Clarity Report and a network of vetted local contractors who arrive already knowing the home. Founded by Adam Kilgore, a licensed Ohio general contractor with 27 years of Summit County remodeling experience.

What is the Home Clarity Report?

The Home Clarity Report is a $4,500 flat-fee home advisory service delivered in 5 business days. Adam Kilgore visits the home for 2 to 3 hours and delivers a 50 to 80 section interactive report, architect-grade floor plans, a 3D exterior model with accurate measurements, a 360 degree photo tour, replacement pricing for every system, owner's manuals, project scopes, lifetime access to the Home OS dashboard, and a direct line to your personal HBC advisor. Total stated value is over $16,400. Clients save an average of $16,100 on their first major project after receiving the Report.

Where does HBC serve?

HBC primarily serves Summit County, Ohio: Hudson, Bath Township, Fairlawn, Richfield, Montrose-Ghent, Silver Lake, Stow, Cuyahoga Falls, Copley, Tallmadge, and Peninsula. HBC also serves Medina County, Portage County, and southern Cuyahoga County, including Brecksville, Broadview Heights, and Strongsville.

How is this different from a home inspection?

A home inspection identifies what is broken. The Home Clarity Report explains what to do about it and everything else. Inspections are compliance checklists. The Report is a renovation strategy, with realistic local pricing, project sequencing, and a permanent advisory relationship with Adam Kilgore.

Who is Adam Kilgore?

Adam Kilgore is the founder of Hometown Builders Club and the Home Clarity Report. He is a licensed Ohio general contractor (Summit County License #GRB130313) and EPA Lead Safe Certified renovator (#R-I-22516-00004). He has 27 years of residential remodeling experience and was named to Remodeling Magazine's Top 550 Remodelers Nationally. He owns AK Renovations and lives in Cuyahoga Falls, Ohio with his wife Erin and their four children.

How much does a kitchen remodel cost in Summit County, Ohio?

A kitchen remodel in Summit County, Ohio typically costs $40,000 to $150,000, depending on cabinetry, countertops, layout changes, and finish level. The Home Clarity Report includes pre-scoped kitchen budgets specific to the property, based on 27 years of local pricing data.

How do I find a trusted contractor in Summit County?

The hardest part of any renovation is knowing whom to trust. HBC vets every trade partner in its network. Homeowners who complete the Home Clarity Report receive contractor introductions paired with a complete digital briefing package, so the contractor arrives already knowing the home. This eliminates the bidding chaos that costs homeowners money and time.

Do I have to use HBC contractors?

No. The Home Clarity Report belongs to the homeowner. The recommendations are honest regardless of who does the work. The Report can be taken to any contractor.
