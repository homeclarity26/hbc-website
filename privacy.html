Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2026

Who We Are

Hometown Builders Club ("we," "us," or "our") is operated by Hometown Builders Club LLC, based in Cuyahoga Falls, Ohio. We provide home advisory services and connect homeowners with vetted trade partners in Summit County and Northeast Ohio. Our website is www.hometownbuildersclub.com.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide to us directly, including:

  • Name, phone number, and email address when you contact us or book a discovery call
  • Your city or neighborhood and information about your home or project
  • Messages you send through our contact form

We also collect limited technical information automatically, including your IP address, browser type, and pages visited, through Google Analytics (see below).

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to your inquiry and schedule your discovery call
  • Deliver the services you request
  • Send you relevant updates about your project (with your consent)
  • Improve our website and services

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our website. Google Analytics collects anonymized data such as pages visited, time on site, and general geographic location. This data is aggregated and does not identify you personally. You can opt out of Google Analytics by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Calendly

We use Calendly to schedule discovery calls. When you book a call, Calendly collects your name and email address. Please review Calendly's Privacy Policy for details on how they handle your data.

Cookies

Our website uses cookies primarily through Google Analytics. Cookies are small text files stored on your device. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though some features of our site may not function properly without them.

Data Retention

We retain contact information only as long as necessary to provide our services or as required by law. Inquiry data is typically retained for up to three years.

Your Rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information. To make a request, contact us at adam@hometownbuildersclub.com or call (330) 203-1331.

Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of our site after changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
