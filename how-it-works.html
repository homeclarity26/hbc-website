THE PROBLEM

Every other home services platform has the same problem.

Whether it's HomeAdvisor, Angi, a neighbor's recommendation, or a contractor you found on Google, the homeowner always starts the same way. They describe their home from scratch to each new contractor, hoping the person on the other end understands what they need and will charge them a fair price.

Contractors have the opposite problem: they invest hours in site visits and estimates for homeowners who haven't thought through a real budget, don't have a clear scope, and may not be ready to move forward at all.

Both sides are wasting time. Both sides are guessing. HBC fixes this at the source, before any contractor conversation happens.

STEP TWO

What happens when you're ready to move forward on a project.

Once your Report is delivered and you've identified which projects to prioritize, HBC connects you with pre-qualified trade partners from our vetted network. Here's what your contractor receives before the first conversation:

Your floor plans (accurate to ⅛ inch)
Your project scope (written by a 27-year remodeling expert)
Your budget range (based on real Summit County pricing data)
Photos of the relevant spaces and systems
System age and condition data
Your overall project goals and priorities

They don't start from zero. They don't have to guess. They walk in knowing your home already. The result: faster conversations, more accurate bids, fewer change orders, and a significantly higher close rate, which means trade partners stay in our network and keep delivering for HBC clients.

THE FULL PROCESS

From first call to lifetime partnership, exactly what happens.

01

Discovery Call (30 minutes, free)

We talk about your home, your goals, and what's on your project list. No pitch. No pressure. If the Home Clarity Report is the right fit, we book the site visit. If it's not, if you need something simpler, or your situation doesn't call for the full Report. Adam will tell you that too. Free advice is free.

02

On-Site Visit (2 to 3 hours)

Adam walks your entire home. He photographs, measures, and scans everything while asking questions about how you live and what you want each space to do. He inspects every mechanical system. He discusses every project on your list. This is the only part that has to happen in person, and it only has to happen once.

03

Report Delivered (within 5 business days)

Your complete Home Clarity Report is live. The written document, floor plans, 3D model, 360° tour, project scopes, and Home OS dashboard are all delivered. Adam also records a personalized 15 to 20 minute video walkthrough reviewing key findings, priority recommendations, and budget guidance in plain English.

04

Lifetime Partnership

From here on, HBC is your permanent home advisor. Projects, questions, contractor introductions, second opinions, one call, forever. When you're ready to move forward on any project, Adam makes the introduction with your complete home profile already in the contractor's hands.
