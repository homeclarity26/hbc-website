THE HOMEOWNER PROBLEM CONCIERGE SOLVES

Most affluent homeowners in Summit County don't actually have a home maintenance problem. They have a coordination problem.

The cleaners come on Wednesdays. The lawn crew comes on Fridays. The HVAC company calls every spring and fall. The gutter guy is a different number than the snow guy. Three of them texted you this week. One missed an appointment. One sent an invoice you forgot to pay. One you fired last year but he keeps showing up anyway.

None of those problems are about money. You can afford every one of those services. The problem is that running them is a part-time job, and you already have a full-time one.

HBC Concierge is the answer. One person manages every vendor on your behalf. You text us when something needs doing. We dispatch, schedule, supervise, and bill it through. Vendor invoices are passed to you at exact cost, with no markup. Our fee is the management itself, charged monthly. The household runs without your attention.

HOW HBC CONCIERGE IS DIFFERENT

Built for the homeowner, not the vendor.

Most home service companies are vendors trying to sell you their service. HBC is your advisor managing all of them. Three differences make that work.

01

Pure pass-through pricing

Every vendor invoice we coordinate gets passed to you at exact cost. No markup. No commissions. No kickbacks. Our fee is the monthly management fee and nothing else, which means we have no incentive to over-service or recommend the most expensive vendor. Our job is to get you the right vendor at the right price.

02

Adam runs it personally

For 27 years Adam has personally worked in, remodeled, or built about 400 homes across Summit County. He knows which HVAC techs do the job right, which cleaners actually clean, and which contractors return calls. The vendor list is not bought from a directory. It is a network built on a quarter century of working alongside these people.

03

Annual home assessment

Every member gets an annual walk-through of the home with Adam. Systems checked, exterior reviewed, projects identified before they become emergencies. Members get a written maintenance calendar with the items, the months, and the vendors. The household stays ahead of the work instead of always behind it.

HOW THE FIRST 30 DAYS GO

From sign-up to running smoothly, in four steps.

No long sales cycle. No multi-month commitment to start. The first month is the onboarding month, and most members are running on autopilot by week three.

STEP 01

Fit call

A 20-minute call with Adam. We confirm the home is in our service area, talk through what you currently coordinate, and pick a tier. No pressure, no upsell.

STEP 02

Onboarding visit

Adam walks the home in person, 60 to 90 minutes. We document existing vendors, identify gaps, set up your member portal, and produce a written maintenance calendar.

STEP 03

Vendor handoff

We notify your existing vendors that scheduling and billing now route through HBC, or we replace them with vetted alternatives if you prefer. You stop getting their texts.

STEP 04

Run mode

By day 30 the household runs through us. You text one number. Vendors get paid through us, you see one consolidated bill. Quarterly check-ins keep the plan current.
