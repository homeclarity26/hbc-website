Why I Built This

I started in the trades in 1999 with a truck, a tool set, and a commitment to honest work across Summit County. Over 27 years, I've renovated hundreds of kitchens, bathrooms, and first floors — and had thousands of conversations with homeowners about their homes.

The pattern I kept seeing: homeowners spending enormous amounts of money without a clear plan, reliable information, or a trusted advisor to help them navigate the process. They were getting bids they couldn't compare, making decisions without context, and fixing things in the wrong order.

Hometown Builders Club is my answer to that problem. It's a home advisory service built around the idea that your home is your most important asset — and you deserve a professional in your corner, not just a contractor you hire project by project.

The Home Clarity Report is the foundation. The lifetime advisory relationship is the value. And the vetted trade partner network is how we make sure the work actually gets done right.

"Your home should work for your life. My job is to help you see it clearly — before you spend a dollar."

— Adam Kilgore
