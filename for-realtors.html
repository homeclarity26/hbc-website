THE GAP BETWEEN CLOSING DAY AND THE FIRST PROJECT

Your client closes. Then reality sets in. And eventually, they call you.

Your buyer closes on a home. They're excited. Then the list forms, the roof needs attention, the HVAC is aging, the kitchen is from 1994 and they want it gone. They don't have a contractor they trust. They don't know what anything costs. They start calling around, get five different numbers, and end up confused and frustrated.

Six months later, something goes wrong. And because you were the person who sold them the house, there's a piece of their frustration that lands on you, even when it has nothing to do with you.

The Home Clarity Report changes this completely.

When you refer a client to HBC after closing, they get a complete expert assessment of their new home in 5 business days. Honest pricing on every project from someone with no stake in the work. A vetted trade network already briefed on their home. And a lifetime advisor in Adam Kilgore, one call, forever, for every question. Your client feels taken care of. You're the person who made it happen. That's a referral relationship that generates business for years.

THE PARTNERSHIP

What the HBC Realtor Partnership looks like in practice.

BENEFIT 01

Free to Join

No fee. No financial commitment. No exclusivity requirement. We partner with realtors who close consistently in the $300K to $800K range in Summit County, and we do it because it works for both sides.

BENEFIT 02

Call Adam During Showings

Partners get direct access to Adam for quick cost estimates during active showings. Walking a home and wondering what the kitchen renovation would run? Text Adam. You'll have a real number in minutes, not days. That kind of on-call expertise makes you look sharper than any competitor.

BENEFIT 03

Co-Branded Marketing Materials

We produce co-branded one-pagers and postcards for your clients, your name alongside HBC. Your buyers see you as the realtor who goes further than anyone else. We handle the design and production.

BENEFIT 04

Referral Fees

For qualified referrals that convert to a Home Clarity Report, partners earn a referral fee. Specific terms are discussed on our 20-minute partnership call, but the short version is: your clients get better service and you get compensated for the introduction.

THE HBC STAMP

Two stamps your listings carry that no other home in Summit County has.

When a homeowner you've worked with goes through HBC, their home earns the Hometown Builders Club stamp and the Home Clarity Report stamp. Those stamps travel with the house. The next time it lists, your client's listing is the one with documented proof of care.

The stamp lifts the listing.

Buyers comparing two homes in the same neighborhood will pick the one with documented systems, scope, and condition over the one with an unknown story. The HBC stamp is the proof. You can use it directly in your listing copy and your open house materials.

The Hub stays with the home.

Every HBC home has a private digital Hub: floor plans, system records, project history, the works. When the home sells, the Hub transfers to the new owner. That's a benefit you can hand to a buyer that no competing listing offers.

EITHER DOOR. BOTH WIN.

However the homeowner finds us, you stay in the picture.

We will never recommend another realtor to a homeowner who already has one. If your client comes to us, you stay in the loop. If a homeowner finds us first and doesn't have a realtor yet, we connect them with one of our partners. Either way, the partnership stands.

If they come to you first.

Refer them in for a Home Clarity Report. We loop you into every conversation, send you the briefing summary, and make sure your client knows you're the reason they got the kind of help most homeowners never see. The 5-business-day Report is delivered in time for offer or post-close planning, whichever you need.

If they come to us first.

If a homeowner contacts HBC without a realtor, we tell them they need one and we send them to a partner from our trusted realtor list. You only get the call if you're already a partner. That's the entire reason this list exists.

THE LOGIC

Why realtors who partner with HBC never go back to doing without it.

Most post-close referrals feel like a favor, you hand a client a contractor's name and hope for the best. With HBC, you're giving your client something with real, documented value: a complete expert assessment of their new home, delivered in 5 business days, with a lifetime advisor included.

The client experience is dramatically better. The perception of you as the realtor who went beyond the transaction is real and lasting. And the next time they need a referral, or know someone buying a home, you're the first call they make. That's the compounding return on a free partnership.
